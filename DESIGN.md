# NeuroZehn design direction

User-pinned direction: clean contemporary medical/research publishing, inspired by SimonMed and SAHMRI without copying their content or pictures. All design decisions delegated by the user. Replace the old serif/olive world with warm white, ink navy, cobalt and restrained gold. Manrope becomes the primary display and reading voice. Urdu uses Noto Naskh Arabic.

Hero: bold left-aligned headline, full institute descriptor, two actions, original abstract blue/amber connection sculpture. The supplied figures are content specifications, rebuilt as selectable text and responsive code-native diagrams: wordmark, journey, roadmap, programme relationships, sourced facts and credentials. Avoid rasterised text, misleading anatomy, invented results, fake portraits or imitation imaging services. Resources and publications remain readily discoverable.

Reference observations: SimonMed offers audience-led task paths and confident sans-serif hierarchy; SAHMRI connects broad institutional topics through rich editorial navigation. Adapt those principles, retaining the client's scope and stage labels. Motion supports journey selection, progression, navigation and artwork; reduced motion removes movement. Maintain keyboard controls, visible focus, mobile layouts and readable source citations.

Audit refinement, 2 November 2026: retain that identity while separating institutional storytelling, discovery libraries and focused task/reading pages. Compact library introductions expose search and results sooner; native disclosures retain longer approved context. Contact begins with one address, response expectation and the form. Course and updates interest require only the details needed for initial contact, with professional background available as optional information. The twelve-programme network uses structured named links around one shared institute hub; no new scientific relationship is implied. Planned topics remain non-clickable until content exists. A grouped mobile drawer, visible keyboard focus and reduced-motion support govern interactions.

The hero and journey concepts remain in `design/concepts/neurozehn/`. The audit authorises tighter type, a smaller header, compact task pages and removal of redundant display copy. The supplied scientific material, actual availability and full institute descriptor take precedence over generated mockup text. Code-native diagrams preserve selectable labels and useful next actions.
