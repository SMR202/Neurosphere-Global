Lead, NeuroSphere Global

SB
Dr. Shahid Bashir, PhD

20+ years of international neuroscience research experience across Switzerland, the United States, Singapore, South Korea, and Saudi Arabia.

PhD in Neurophysiology (University of Fribourg, under Prof. Eric M. Rouiller); postdoctoral training at Harvard Medical School (under Prof. Alvaro Pascual-Leone); Research Lead at King Fahad Specialist Hospital – Dammam; academic affiliation at King Saud University.

210+ peer-reviewed publications; serves on two ILAE Diagnostic Methods Commission task forces and the ILAE EEG Platform Initiative for the Eastern Mediterranean Region.

Research spans non-invasive brain stimulation (TMS, tDCS, tACS), cortical neurophysiology, rare genetic epilepsies, neurodevelopmental disorders, neurodegeneration, stroke rehabilitation, and global burden of disease (GBD) epidemiological modeling.
